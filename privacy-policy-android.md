# WasHere Android 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

更新日期 / Last updated: 2026-11-01

## 中文

WasHere 是一款记录户外活动和轨迹的应用。本政策介绍 Android 版本如何处理信息。如有问题，请联系 **qiuxincsuhk@hotmail.com**。

### 位置与运动记录

经你授权后，应用使用设备的位置显示当前位置、获取天气，并在你开始运动记录后记录轨迹。为了在锁屏或切换应用时继续记录，应用会在记录期间使用前台定位服务；根据系统版本和你的授权，后台定位权限也可能用于支持持续记录。你可以在 Android 系统设置中更改位置和通知权限。

运动记录可能包含位置坐标、时间、距离、速度、方向、定位精度、海拔及相关天气数据。记录默认保存在设备本地，开发者不通过自有服务器收集或查看你的运动轨迹。

### 地图和天气服务

为提供地图和天气，设备需要直接连接第三方服务。这些服务可能处理 IP 地址、设备及网络信息，以及提供服务所需的位置或地图区域信息；具体处理方式以服务商政策为准。

- **高德地图**：在中国大陆用于显示地图；应用会在启用前展示相关隐私说明。[高德隐私政策](https://lbs.amap.com/pages/privacy/)。
- **Mapbox**：在其他地区用于显示地图。[Mapbox 隐私政策](https://www.mapbox.com/legal/privacy/)。
- **Open-Meteo**：用于查询天气。请求中的经纬度保留两位小数，约为公里级精度；服务商也可能收到 IP 地址。[Open-Meteo 隐私信息](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms)。

### 备份、导出与分享

你可以导出 GPX 轨迹或活动记录备份，并选择设备存储或系统文件选择器提供的其他位置。如果你选择云盘或其他第三方应用，文件会交由所选服务处理；请参阅该服务的隐私政策。当前版本不应被理解为已提供自动云同步。

Android 系统备份也可能根据设备设置、系统版本和备份服务的规则备份应用数据。备份是否发生及如何管理，请查看设备的系统备份设置。导出或备份后的文件可能包含精确轨迹，请谨慎选择保存位置和分享对象。

### 通知、删除与保留

记录运动时，应用会显示前台服务通知；若开启相关功能，也可能显示本地里程提醒。你可以在应用或系统设置中管理可用的通知选项。

你可以在应用中删除运动记录。卸载应用通常会删除该设备上的应用本地数据；你另行导出的文件、云盘文件或系统备份可能仍然存在，需要在对应位置管理或删除。

### 儿童与政策更新

本应用不以儿童为专门对象。如果本政策发生变化，我们会更新本页面及上方日期。有关个人信息的问题或请求，请发送邮件至 **qiuxincsuhk@hotmail.com**。

## English

WasHere is an app for recording outdoor activities and routes. This policy describes how the Android app handles information. Contact: **qiuxincsuhk@hotmail.com**.

### Location and activity records

With your permission, the app uses device location to show your position, obtain weather and record a route after you start an activity. During recording, a foreground location service supports continued recording when the screen is locked or you switch apps. Depending on your Android version and permissions, background location access may also support ongoing recording. You can change location and notification permissions in Android settings.

Activity records may include coordinates, timestamps, distance, speed, bearing, location accuracy, elevation and associated weather. Records are stored locally on your device by default. The developer does not collect or view your routes through a developer-operated server.

### Maps and weather

Your device connects directly to third-party services to provide maps and weather. These services may process IP address, device and network information, and the location or map area needed to provide their services. Their own policies govern their processing.

- **AMap** provides maps in mainland China; the app presents a privacy notice before enabling it. [AMap privacy policy](https://lbs.amap.com/pages/privacy/).
- **Mapbox** provides maps in other regions. [Mapbox privacy policy](https://www.mapbox.com/legal/privacy/).
- **Open-Meteo** provides weather. The app sends coordinates rounded to two decimal places, approximately kilometre-level precision; the service may also receive your IP address. [Open-Meteo terms and privacy information](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms).

### Backup, export and sharing

You can export GPX routes or activity backups and choose device storage or another destination offered by the system file picker. If you choose a cloud drive or another third-party app, that service handles the file under its own privacy policy. The current version should not be understood as offering automatic cloud sync.

Android system backup may also back up app data depending on your device settings, Android version and backup provider. Review your device's backup settings to manage it. Exported or backed-up files may contain precise routes, so choose their destinations and recipients carefully.

### Notifications, retention and deletion

The app displays a foreground service notification while recording and may show local distance reminders when enabled. You can manage available notification options in the app or system settings.

You can delete activity records in the app. Uninstalling generally removes local app data from the device. Files you exported, cloud files and system backups may remain and must be managed or deleted in their respective locations.

### Children and updates

The app is not specifically directed at children. If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. For privacy questions or requests, email **qiuxincsuhk@hotmail.com**.
