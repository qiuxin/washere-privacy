## 常见问题

### WasHere 是否需要注册账号？
不需要。WasHere 无需注册账号即可使用。

### 为什么需要位置权限？
WasHere 使用位置权限来显示你的位置并记录 GPS 运动轨迹。部分轨迹记录功能需要持续访问位置。

### GPS 轨迹不准确怎么办？
GPS 精度可能受到建筑物、天气、室内环境和设备信号等因素影响。建议在室外开阔区域使用，并确保已允许 WasHere 使用定位服务。

WasHere is a simple, privacy-focused outdoor activity and GPS track recording app for walking, hiking, running, and exploring.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need an account?
No. WasHere does not require an account to use the app.

### Why does WasHere need location access?
WasHere uses location access to show your current location and record your GPS tracks. Some track-recording features require continuous location access.

### Why is my GPS track inaccurate?
GPS accuracy may be affected by buildings, weather, indoor environments, and device signal conditions. For better accuracy, use WasHere outdoors in an open area and make sure location services are enabled.
